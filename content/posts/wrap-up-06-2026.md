New section! This is data from the Oura ring that tracks one's sleep. Both scores are at the scale fo 0 to 100. I am not saying the score of 80 is bad, but I know when I get a good sleep the sleep score is usually 85-90, so an average of <80 just means I have a minor to moderate sleep issue. I used to have an old Oura ring but it needs charging frequently and misses some of the new features like cardiovascular age estimation, so I bought a new gen 5 Oura ring in June. Then I discovered Oura have APIs so I just download some of my data here.

I was already aware of the sleep issue before - I usually fall asleep ok, but wake up quite early. When I do that, I feel quite tired immediately after the wake up, and would feel so throughout the day. What I tried in June was to sleep earlier so that even if I wake up early the total duration is ok. It works out sometimes, but one issue of sleeping early is sometimes I am not sleepy enough by the bed time. As mentioned before, reading Money Stuff (The column) sometimes helps. I will keep this one monitored and also add some more data from new features.
