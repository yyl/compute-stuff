The numbers didn’t do justice because I forgot wearing my watch twice when surfing, so the actual times I went surfing is 5 this month. This is substantial. In the past, my surfing frequency has been once every two months - surfing as an exercise itself costs way more time compared to weightlifting etc. There’s a lot more preparation and cleanup (wetsuit, surfboard), longer commute (40min to beach), and longer session (I usually surf for 2 hours). Finally, I also have to wait for a suitable surf condition - for beginners like me powerful waves or strong wind would be brutal. 

How’s this time different? Well, 3 things line up together:
1. My vacations days are accumulating too much - I haven’t taken days off for quite a while
2. I am close to burn-out at work
3. Surfing condition has been decent

So I just take weekdays off to surf, and devoted most of my personal time on it. It is pretty great!  Now I won’t spend the whole wrap up post on surfing - will reserve another post (hopefully) on surfing - but consider this as an excuse of why I didn’t write more posts!
