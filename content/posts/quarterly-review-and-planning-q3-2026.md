The title sounds very official and corporate, but I can’t think of a better one at the moment, so we’ll keep it as is. The purpose is to review my Q3 goals and plan for Q4.

Overall, I give myself a __B-__ for Q3 and have four new goals for Q4. Read on for more details.

## Grading Q3 goals

For people who work at a company (large or small), setting quarterly goals should sound familiar (or, more officially, OKRs). I’ve started applying this practice to my personal life as a way to better capture and understand what I’ve accomplished. Time sometimes flies by so fast that I don’t even know where it went!

### O1(health): Improve average sleep score to 80+
* Result: quarterly average sleep score 76.6
* Grade: C
 
My Q2 score was actually 77.6, so this is a small decline. Subjectively, if I had to recall, I’d say my sleep improved during the first half of the quarter. But towards the end, things got a bit hectic due to unexpected interview prep, and I got a bit stressed, causing my sleep quality to decline.

### O2(family): Prepare for the big trip
* Result: 70% checklist is complete
* Grade: B
 
We have a big trip coming up in Q4, so we need to prepare quite a few things, including getting the proper travel documents and certifications, finding a dog sitter, etc. Most of these are done, and the remaining items aren't particularly time-sensitive, except for the dog sitter. Our usual dog sitter isn't available, and we haven't found a replacement yet. I need to lock one in sooner rather than later, since fewer sitters will be available as the trip gets closer.

### O3(exercise): surf 3 times, zone 2 training 10 times
* Result: surf 8 times, zone 2 training 5 times
* Grade: A
 
When I was setting this goal, I was under a bit of a time crunch and hadn't surfed for a long time. So I figured surfing 3 times would be more than enough, and I should focus on Zone 2 training because it's easier logistically (I can do it at home) and easier on my body.
But then I ended up finding more time windows for surfing and went 8 times—more than I did in the entire previous year! I also ended up doing much less Zone 2 training than planned.
So the results are basically inverted, but I'm still quite happy because surfing matters a lot more to me. I enjoy it much more, and it's also a great form of exercise.

### O4(write): Produce 6 blog posts
* Result: wrote 5
* Grade: B

This is the most difficult goal after sleep because maintaining writing as a habit is so challenging. Not only do I have to come up with topics and spend time writing, but the writing process itself can be a real grind. I don't know about others, but I definitely struggle with it.
Moreover, most of my posts are about things I've done, but I really don't take enough notes while doing them, which makes writing about them afterwards even harder.
Nonetheless, I was so close to achieving this goal until interviews disrupted things towards the end of the quarter, leaving me with 5 posts.

### O5(read): Finish the book AEIUO
* Result: 70% complete
* Grade: B

Another goal that was disrupted by interviews, since I stopped reading when interview prep took over.

### Overall: B-

I fell short of most goals, ending up around 70–80% complete on several of them, while also falling short on the most important one (sleep). So instead of a B, I'd say it's more like a B-. What I've learned, though, is that maybe my sleep goal was just too ambitious, given how difficult improving sleep can be.

Setting goals is still a useful practice, though, because it:
1. Helps me focus (and give up everything else that falls outside my goals).
2. Gives me an opportunity to revisit my quarter with a clearer record of what I've done.

## Q4 goals

### O1(health): keep an average score of 76

We're already halfway through October, and my sleep has been bad lately—averaging just 73. Considering we have a big trip coming up, along with the holidays when schedules can fluctuate quite a bit, I'm setting a more conservative goal this time.

### O2(family): wrap up trip planning

This is the second most important goal in Q4 because it's now time-sensitive. We need to find a dog sitter and wrap up the remaining planning, such as deciding on places to visit, buying gifts, etc.

### O3(exercise): show ~5% incremental gains

I'm finding that simply counting exercise sessions isn't particularly useful, because:
1. Exercise is something I should and want to do regularly anyway, just like work (and for the same reason, simply counting hours worked is meaningless).
2. As a goal, counting sessions doesn't make much sense because there's limited room for improvement—I can't keep increasing my exercise frequency indefinitely.
3. 
Instead, in Q4, I'll try to track and demonstrate some measurable gains in both weightlifting and surfing. I still need to work out the exact metrics.


### O4(read): finish the book AEIUO

Effort-wise, this should be a small goal, but for completeness, I want to finish the book.

Overall, Q4 is a shorter quarter because of the holidays, so I'm trying to be conservative with my goals. Let's finish the year strong! I'm excited to revisit my 2026 goals at the end of the year.
